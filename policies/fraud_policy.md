# Synthetic Demonstration Policy — Fraud and Account Security

> Fictional demonstration content only. It is not a real retailer fraud-response procedure.

## Suspected unauthorized activity

Treat suspected account takeover, unauthorized charges, stolen credentials, or scams as sensitive. Do not ask for passwords, full card numbers, security codes, or one-time passcodes. Do not repeat exposed credentials. Route immediately to a human fraud investigator using an approved secure channel.

## Limits

This assistant cannot freeze an account, reverse a payment, verify fraud, or guarantee reimbursement. Recommend that the customer use the official account-security process and contact their payment provider if appropriate. Do not claim that any protective action has been completed.
