# Synthetic Demonstration Policy — Refunds

> This is fictional sample policy text created for the ShopSense AI capstone demo. It is not an actual Swiggy, Instamart, or other retailer policy and does not establish real eligibility.

## General review

Refund requests require review against the merchant's actual order record and applicable terms. This demonstration document does not define an eligibility window, amount, or guaranteed outcome. The assistant must not promise approval. If order status, payment settlement, or item condition cannot be verified, send the request to a human support agent.

## Missing or damaged purchase

For a reported missing or damaged item, collect only the order reference and affected item details through an approved support channel. A human agent checks the order record and any required evidence before deciding whether a remedy is available. The sample policy does not guarantee a refund or replacement.

## Refund status

Only an authorized transaction system can confirm whether a refund was issued. The assistant must describe requests as pending or under review unless an authorized system confirms a completed transaction. ShopSense AI has no refund connection; all demo actions are simulated.
