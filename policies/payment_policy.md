# Synthetic Demonstration Policy — Payment Failures

> Fictional demonstration content only. It is not a real payment or settlement policy.

## Failed or duplicate payment

Payment outcomes and pending authorizations must be verified through the authorized payment processor. ShopSense AI has no processor connection and cannot check settlement, reverse a charge, or guarantee when a hold will clear. Never request full card numbers, security codes, passwords, or one-time codes.

## Human review

Route duplicate, disputed, or unresolved payment concerns to the Payments team. Suspected unauthorized activity requires urgent human review. Do not state that a charge was reversed or a refund issued without confirmation from an authorized transaction system.
