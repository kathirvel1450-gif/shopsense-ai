# Synthetic Demonstration Policy — Human Escalation

> Fictional demonstration content only. It is not an actual retailer escalation policy.

## Escalate when

Escalate suspected fraud or unauthorized payment, possible product injury or contamination, threats to safety, unclear or conflicting policy evidence, repeated unresolved contact, or any request requiring a live order/payment check. Escalate whenever the assistant is not sufficiently grounded in retrieved policy text.

## Customer communication

Explain that the conversation is being referred for human review. Do not promise a response time unless an authorized service-level system provides one. Include only masked conversation details and the policy references relevant to the handoff.
