# Synthetic Demonstration Policy — Damaged Products

> Fictional demonstration content only. It is not an actual retailer policy.

## Damage report

Ask the customer to identify the affected item and describe the damage. Do not request unnecessary personal information. If evidence is needed, the customer should submit it through an approved support channel rather than placing sensitive information in chat.

## Safety and remedy

Potential injury, contamination, illness, or other product safety concern requires immediate human review. A human agent checks applicable merchant terms and order facts. This sample document does not promise a refund or replacement.
